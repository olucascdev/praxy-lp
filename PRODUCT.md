# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js with React, selected explicitly by the user. The landing page is a single route and includes the official Cal.com React embed.

## Users

Inferred from the supplied brief: business and technology leaders evaluating a unified AI infrastructure partner, especially teams that need to move from AI experimentation to dependable operation.

## Product Purpose

Praxy presents an integrated infrastructure layer for building, shipping, and scaling AI systems, then converts qualified interest into a scheduled “Protocolo Praxy” conversation.

## Positioning

Inferred: a unified operational protocol connects AI strategy, implementation, governance, and measurable business outcomes instead of presenting isolated automation tools.

## Operating Context

Visitors discover the platform through a cinematic marketing page, inspect department use cases and the Praxy protocol, review illustrative outcome metrics, and schedule a consultation through Cal.com.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Mobile-first responsive landing page with a desktop composition calibrated to 1487 × 1058.
- CloudFront video URL supplied by the user is the required hero media.
- Cal.com namespace and event link are `protocolo-praxy` and `praxy/protocolo-praxy`.
- The first viewport stays free of stats, cards, badges, and secondary marketing blocks.
- Commercial proof values were not supplied; displayed metrics must be identified as illustrative.

## Brand Commitments

Praxy. Black-space backgrounds, white/silver typography, acid yellow `#ffff3c`, an abstract bolt/S mark, restrained glass, Fira Sans display, Lora details, Inter descriptions, and Manrope for the original hero UI language.

## Evidence on Hand

Five visual references supplied in the conversation define composition and pacing. No customer logos, testimonials, case-study photography, or verified performance claims were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make advanced AI infrastructure feel legible and operational.
- Demonstrate a connected system rather than a collection of tools.
- Keep primary actions unambiguous and easy to reach.
- Treat motion, contrast, and responsive behavior as functional design materials.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Honor reduced-motion preferences, keyboard navigation, semantic landmarks, visible focus, accessible mobile navigation, and readable contrast.
